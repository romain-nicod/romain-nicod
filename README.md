### Romain Nicod

I set up and run the machinery that makes delivery predictable: PMOs, portfolio
governance, operating models, vendor and outsourcing management. Since 2008,
across public health, online betting, gaming and crypto. Independent since 2025,
under [AI-GMENTED.pm](https://ai-gmented.pm).

**Hands-on when needed, hands-off when ready, gone when done.**

Same job, new constraint: teams where humans and AI agents deliver together.
I govern my own work that way before I sell it to anyone. Written rules, a
destructiveness scale that decides what needs a backup and what needs a second
pair of eyes, a request register per project where an item is only ticked once
its result has been checked. The repositories below are that method, published
whole.

- 📐 [kickoff](https://github.com/romain-nicod/kickoff) carries the rules
  themselves: golden rules, definition of ready and done, code hygiene, secret
  handling. Open them before you take my word for any of this.
- ⚒️ [Skill Forge](https://forge.ai-gmented.pm) turns a five-minute
  questionnaire into a Claude skill fitted to your own context. No account, no
  API key, nothing leaves your browser.
- 🎯 I have mentored product managers, product owners and scrum masters, and I
  still teach.
- ✍️ I write about AI governance, delivery and product craft on
